## Labour-law decisions

### L01 — Applicable law and current instrument set — Blocked

Propose an explicit applicability record per employment/rule, including employee status, s19 hours,
sectoral/bargaining instruments and effective dates. Alternative: assume BCEA default for everyone;
reject that assumption. S01/S02/S04; #22/#78/#85–#88. Need certified amendment/commencement and later-
order search, missing full Bill and current UIF material. Impact: versioned source/applicability
catalogue and NeedsReview result. Reviewer role: qualified South African labour-law practitioner.

### L02 — Annual cycles, method, fractions and service continuity — Blocked

Propose employment-anniversary cycles and explicit agreed 1:17 day/hour method where applicable;
otherwise reviewed consecutive-period equivalent. Decide leap-day anniversaries, pro-rata opening,
paid qualifying records, interruptions, carry-forward and lawful variations. Alternatives: anniversary
versus approved special transition, exact rational versus bounded decimal precision; never January
reset/automatic forfeiture by default. S01 ss20–21/40/49–50, S05; #22/#85. Reviewer role: labour-law
practitioner with company HR/payroll policy owner; no payroll engine is authorised.

### L03 — Sick cycle and first-six-month inputs — Researched

Located 36-month/six-week and actual-worked 1:26 text; propose distinct earned/use counters so early
use is deducted once. Decide changing-pattern six-week reference, cycle interruptions, fractional
worked days and missing records. Alternative scheduled-day estimate is not evidence of actual work.
S01 s22(1)–(6), S05; #22/#86. Impact: provenance and unavailable calculation instead of invented zero.
Reviewer role: labour-law practitioner. Researched does not approve boundary examples.

### L04 — Sick episode, proof, exceptions and payment review — Blocked

Propose separately recorded absence, employer request, evidence sufficiency and pay review. Decide
consecutive-day/weekend/occasion semantics, rolling eight-week endpoints, practitioner verification,
assistance and occupational-compensation exceptions; no diagnosis collection. Alternatives: universal
certificate gate versus reviewed conditional workflow; reject universal submission gate. S01 ss23–24,
S05; #84/#86/#22. Impact: Required Medical stays unsupported pending usable absence/payment separation.
Reviewer role: labour-law practitioner; qualified medical advice only where issuer/fitness requires it.

### L05 — Fixed/part-time schedules, holidays and precision — Blocked

Propose immutable weekly revisions and holiday-source versions, per-date charging explanation and
explicit exchanges. Decide schedule-change reference, day/hour conversion, leap/month arithmetic,
fractions and one-time rounding boundary; unsupported shifts return NeedsReview. Alternative:
calendar-only or nominal eight-hour days cannot silently claim statutory correctness. S01 ss18/20(8),
S02 s2, S06; #78 holiday-source remainder/#22/#85–#88. Reviewer role: labour-law practitioner with
company schedule/holiday owner. #78 being Closed does not erase its unmet holiday-source criterion.

### L06 — Family events, service and enhancement boundary — Researched

Located more-than-four-month/four-days-week eligibility, statutory event list, three-day cycle,
part-day/proof/lapse/variation clauses. Approve exact service boundary, changed/irregular schedules,
proportionate part-day charge and reasonable proof before pay. Alternative expanded compassionate
leave must be a C01 enhancement, not statutory relabelling. S01 s27, S02 2002 s4/2018 s4, S05;
#87/#22. Reviewer role: labour-law practitioner and HR policy owner for enhancements.

### L07 — Van Wyk authority, adoption age and transition — Blocked

Propose retain explicit InterimCourtOrder source version. Resolve order 3/4 versus order 5(c)'s
retained age text, later supplementary orders, pre-/post-order events, pending applications and
future legislation's transitional clauses. Alternatives include continued suspended-age restriction
versus broader entitlement: engineer must not choose. S03 order 2–7, S04; #88/#22. Impact: no age gate
or automatic date-based migration until approved. Reviewer role: constitutional/labour-law practitioner.

### L08 — Shared allocation and calendar-period arithmetic — Blocked

Propose event-linked parental case, minimal declaration of external employment/allocation, separate
month-period and additional-day components. Resolve common anchor, different employers, both-employed
versus sole-employed/self-employed, near-half odd-day split, concurrent consumption, disagreement
completion window and single-sequence application across s25/B/C. Alternatives: append ten days to
each parent or fixed 120-day pool are rejected; agreed versus default allocations require distinct
rules. S03 order 5(a) s25(1)/(4A–D), 5(c)/(d); #88/#22. Reviewer role: labour-law practitioner.

### L09 — Recovery, notice and safe work — Researched

Located four-week pre-birth, six-week fitness/loss protections, exceptional notice, adoption earlier
placement/order, commissioning birth and s26 support. Approve birth-health facts, continuity, return
fitness, inability to notify and employment protection workflow; notification lateness must not
silently extinguish statutory rights. S01 s26; S03 s25(2–6), s25B(2–4), s25C(2–4); #88/#86.
Reviewer role: labour-law practitioner; privacy reviewer approves health-data design separately.

### L10 — Family facts and non-gender eligibility — Proposed

Propose relationship/responsibility/event declarations and limited HR review, not a gender or marital-
status switch. Resolve necessary evidence and treatment of external parent's changed employment,
multiple births/adoptions and disrupted placements. S03 order 5 s25(4D)/s25B/C; S08; #88/#84.
Impact: do not infer case history from existing profiles. Reviewer role: labour-law practitioner.

### L11 — Employer pay versus UIF — Blocked

Propose time-off entitlement and employer-pay status distinct from informational UIF referral.
No benefit award/duration engine. Need current UI Act/regulations/commencement and authoritative
benefit-administration guidance; S07 retrieval incomplete. S02/S03 do not authorise equalised interim
UIF payments. Alternative promising automatic full pooled pay is rejected. #86–#88/#22.
Reviewer role: labour-law/UIF specialist; company payroll owner for company-paid enhancements.

## Company-policy decisions

### C01 — Enhancements and unpaid mode — Proposed

Effective-dated separately labelled company grants, with explicit paid/unpaid/no-consumption mode;
review lawful floors and governing agreements. Zero entitlement is not unpaid mode. Alternative
reuse statutory balances obscures provenance and is rejected. S01 ss19(2)/20(6)/49–50, L01/L11;
#22/#85–#88. Reviewer role: authorised company HR policy owner, labour-law check where affecting rights.

### C02 — Pending reservations, overlaps and reversals — Proposed

Keep no Pending reservation initially. Separate overlap permission from lawful incompatibility and
from reporting unique-person metrics. Approve future Approved cancellation/change authority,
append-only charge reversal and handling of already taken leave. Alternative automatic refund/
historical overwrite rejected. L02/L04, S01 s20(5–7); #22. Existing owner Pending cancellation and
HR deactivation cancellation do not authorise cancellation of Approved records. Reviewer role:
company HR policy owner with labour-law practitioner for rights/overlap implications.

### C03 — Cutover and unknown historical charge facts — Proposed

Prefer prospective reviewed cutover plus separately evidenced opening entries. Alternatives: keep
legacy model until complete records, or explicit manual reconciliation with employee dispute path.
Never backcalculate old approvals using today's schedule. Preserve reports on original calendar basis.
L01/L02/L05, S08; #22/#78/#85. Reviewer role: company HR/data owner with labour-law and privacy reviewers.

## Privacy and retention decisions

### P01 — Minimum facts, medical access and third-party declarations — Blocked

Propose purpose-specific data dictionary, lawful basis and confidential owner/authorised-HR medical
content; Manager status only. Specify whether each attachment is necessary, employee/child/other-parent
notice, external-employer declaration reliability and correction. No diagnosis/gender proxy or medical
summary export. S08 ss10–13/18/26–27/32/34–35/71–72; #84/#86–#88/#22.
Reviewer role: Information Officer/privacy counsel plus labour-law reviewer of necessity.

### P02 — Record-class retention, legal holds and recovery — Blocked

Propose separate request/charge/audit, evidence, access-log, notification, image and backup schedules;
hold authority/release, restricted processing and disposal proof. Alternatives indefinite storage or
a single guessed statutory period are rejected. Determine applicable employment/claims duties and
POPIA s14; restored expired data and holds need recovery procedures. S01 s31, S08 ss14/19–22;
#81/#82/#83/#22. Reviewer role: Information Officer/privacy and labour-law counsel; operations owner
executes only an approved policy. No purge or hold feature is implemented by this register.
