# Worked-example catalogue for qualified review

Prepared **20 October 2026**. All employee/case inputs are hypothetical, not real personal data.
These are review proposals, **not approved legal results or automated statutory tests**. Numeric
arithmetic may be checked independently without approving entitlement. For every example, review
status is **Pending review**, reviewer **Unassigned**, decision date/sign-off **blank**.
Sources S01–S08 and decision IDs refer to [requirements](04-south-african-leave-requirements.md)
and the [decision register](11-south-african-rule-decisions.md).

Dates below are South African local calendar dates. A half-open interval `[start, nextStart)` is
proposed for internal cycle arithmetic; a leave form still has inclusive start/end dates. A day
is not necessarily eight hours; minutes below come only from explicit example schedules.

## E04 — Part-time holidays and weekend substitution

Inputs: Mon/Wed/Fri four hours; 2026-04-27 through 2026-05-01. Steps: five calendar dates; three normal
scheduled dates; Freedom Day and Workers' Day remove Mon/Fri from the proposed annual charge.
Proposed result: **one scheduled day/240 minutes**, not five or a nominal eight-hour day. An ordinary
three-week span has nine such scheduled days before holiday adjustments; it is not a universal
15-day entitlement. Separately, Sunday 2026-08-09 also gives Monday 10 August; Saturday 2026-03-21
creates no automatic Monday 23 March holiday. Sunday workers require evaluation of the original
holiday too. Holiday exchanges require recorded agreement, not manual silent deletion. S06 ss2/2A,
S01 s18/20(8), L05; #78/#22/#85. Entitlement conversion remains Pending review.
